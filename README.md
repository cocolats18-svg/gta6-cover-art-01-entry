Vice City Cover Maker is a multi-panel GTA 6-style cover maker that runs entirely offline in a desktop browser. Load your own photos, fill three panels with one photo or several, crop and position each one, and write and edit the bold caption over a Vice City sunset and neon colour grade. Your photos stay on your machine — nothing is uploaded — and the finished cover exports as a PNG in the common post sizes 1080x1080, 1080x1350, and 1920x1080.

The design is original: no Rockstar Games logo, wordmark, official artwork, character, or font file. Fonts are DejaVu Sans, shipped under app/fonts with its license.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Or serve the app itself for a try in the browser with `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
